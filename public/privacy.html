MathKhata Public Beta

Privacy

MathKhata is local-first. It has no account system, advertising, analytics beacon, telemetry, or cloud notebook database.

Notebook storage

Your pages are stored in this browser's local IndexedDB database. They leave the app only when you deliberately export, copy, or upload them. Clearing browser site data can remove notebooks, so export important work.

Microphone and speech

Voice input starts only after you open Voice and grant microphone permission. Browser speech recognition may use the browser vendor's speech service. If recognition is unavailable or permission is denied, MathKhata reports that honestly and creates no fabricated transcript.

Mathematics and assistant

Deterministic parsing, page grouping, calculations, and supported CAS operations run in the browser. The public beta does not send notebook text to OpenAI or another hosted AI model and does not connect to a visitor's localhost model. The optional Local Qwen Assistant is a desktop/local-development feature.

External feedback

If you choose to report an issue through an external tracker, do not include private notebook content. That submission is governed by the tracker's own privacy terms.
