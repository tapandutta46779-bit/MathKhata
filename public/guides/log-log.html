Log-log graphs

Log-log axes are useful when both variables span several orders of magnitude or when a power law is expected. Each decade is marked clearly while minor grid lines stay quiet.

Try this workflow

  1. Open Research → Log-Log Graph.
  2. Add a positive-data series or a power-law expression such as y=x^2.
  3. Pan and zoom to inspect decades, then hover or tap intersections to mark coordinates.
  4. Copy the independent graph snapshot to a notebook page with its equations and viewport.

Logarithmic axes require positive x and y values. Invalid or incomplete data is called out in the expression rail.
