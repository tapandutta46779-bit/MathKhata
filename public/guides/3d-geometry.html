Explore 3D geometry

Research → 3D Geometry combines structured constructions with an orbitable local canvas. Start with a point, then add a cube, cuboid, sphere, ellipsoid, cylinder, cone, paraboloid, tetrahedron, or octahedron.

Try this workflow

  1. Choose Point and enter coordinates such as point(1,2,3).
  2. Select an object from the gallery or enter its construction command.
  3. Drag blank space to orbit, Shift-drag to pan, and use the wheel or pinch gesture to zoom.
  4. Select an object for labels, visibility, color, measurements, or a translated/rotated/reflected/dilated copy.

Every research copy is independent: resetting the source workspace does not change a snapshot already placed on a page.
